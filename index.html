Our mission

Cannabis research remains fragmented, with important gaps in evidence for clinical and patient decision-making. The Open Cannabis Research Initiative seeks to expand independent, accessible and scientifically rigorous investigation of cannabinoids, terpenes, formulation chemistry, therapeutic applications, adverse effects and long-term outcomes.

Our objective is not to promote consumption or establish predetermined conclusions, but to empower patients and clinicians with evidence for informed, individualized decisions. PTSD is our initial research priority, not the limit of our scientific interests.

Flagship research program: PTSD

We propose a systematic investigation of chemically characterized cannabinoid formulations, using terpene-free THC as a reference condition. The central hypothesis is that formulation composition may influence tolerability and PTSD-related outcomes, including whether sustained nightmare relief facilitates trauma processing.

This is an investigator-seeking initiative, not an active clinical trial. Patient observations are hypothesis-generating, not evidence of efficacy.

A distributed research initiative

We invite multiple independent institutions to investigate shared research questions, replicate findings and contribute complementary studies. No single institution or sponsor is intended to control the research agenda. Coordinated protocols and independent replication can strengthen the reliability and generalizability of findings.

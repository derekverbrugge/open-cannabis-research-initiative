Cannabinoid formulation research for PTSD

Seeking academic investigators and industry partners to evaluate individual terpene–THC interactions, trauma-related sleep disturbances and functional impairment.

Explore research objectives Partner with us

Research opportunity

We propose a systematic investigation of chemically characterized cannabinoid formulations, using terpene-free THC as a reference condition. The central hypothesis is that formulation composition may influence tolerability and PTSD-related outcomes, including whether sustained nightmare relief facilitates trauma processing.

This is an investigator-seeking initiative, not an active clinical trial. Patient observations are hypothesis-generating, not evidence of efficacy.

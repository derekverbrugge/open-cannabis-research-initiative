Research collaboration

Seeking independent academic and institutional support.

Academic investigators

Academic cannabinoid laboratories, PTSD clinicians, sleep researchers, analytical laboratories, pharmaceutical developers and nonprofit research sponsors are invited to explore the research questions.

We seek principal investigators with expertise in cannabinoid pharmacology, psychiatry, sleep medicine, clinical trial design and biostatistics to refine hypotheses and assess feasibility.

Industry research and development partners

Potential contributions include institutional funding, analytical chemistry, toxicology and standardized formulation expertise. No endorsement, affiliation or funding commitment is claimed.

Collaboration opportunities

  • Investigator-sponsored research funding and grant development
  • Institutional analytical chemistry and formulation characterization
  • Pharmacokinetic, toxicological and delivery-system expertise
  • Independent evidence synthesis and protocol development

No materials are requested for personal use. Any study materials would be handled by authorized research institutions.

Independence

Disclose conflicts of interest and protect independent study design, analysis and publication.

Open participation across institutions

Participation is nonexclusive. Universities, hospitals, independent laboratories and industry research teams are invited to contribute parallel studies, independent replications or complementary research. Multiple sponsors may support distinct institutional projects without controlling publication or interpretation.

We encourage collaboration on common measurement standards, protocol development, reference formulation characterization and evidence synthesis while preserving each institution's independence. Participation does not require affiliation with another participating institution.

The initiative seeks to connect investigators; it does not currently claim an established consortium or confirmed institutional membership.

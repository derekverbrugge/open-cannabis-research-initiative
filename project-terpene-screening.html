Terpene interaction screening

Proposed project — seeking independent investigators.

Research question

Do individual terpenes alter acute THC effects?

Scientific rationale

Patient observations and gaps in existing evidence motivate this hypothesis; efficacy is not established. Review relevant literature.

Proposed methodology

Controlled interaction studies after toxicology review; matched exposure, placebo and terpene-only controls.

Candidate outcomes and limitations

Anxiety, paranoia, adverse events; independent replication.

Collaboration

Multiple institutions may pursue parallel or complementary studies. Investigators determine sample size, endpoints, approvals and analysis plans independently.

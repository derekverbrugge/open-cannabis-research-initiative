Relief and recovery

Patient observations motivate investigation of whether sustained relief from distressing dreams can create psychological space for trauma processing. The initiative also asks whether removing or selectively reintroducing terpenes changes tolerability.

Dream recall is not the same as REM sleep or the absence of dreaming. Individual experiences cannot establish causation or general effectiveness.

Research advocacy only. Cannabis is not an established PTSD treatment; discuss care with a qualified clinician.

Research prospectus

Institutional discussion document — proposed research, not an approved protocol.

Rationale

Investigate whether chemically characterized cannabinoid formulations differ in PTSD-related sleep symptoms, anxiety and functional impairment. Patient observations generate hypotheses, not proof of efficacy.

Research portfolio

  • Terpene interaction screening: Do individual terpenes alter acute THC effects?
  • Nightmares and sleep: Does formulation affect trauma-related nightmares or dream recall?
  • Longitudinal trauma recovery: Is sustained nightmare relief associated with durable recovery?
  • Functional impairment: Do formulations differ in cognitive and psychomotor effects?
  • Formulation comparison: Does chemical composition predict response better than strain labels?
  • Selective terpene reintroduction: Can individual compounds modify tolerability without worsening other outcomes?
  • Treatment discontinuation: What happens to sleep and symptoms after treatment ends?

Institutional model

Independent parallel studies, replication and multisite collaboration with shared measures where appropriate. Sponsors may support institutional research without controlling findings.

Partnership principles

Participation is voluntary and nonexclusive. Institutions retain scientific independence, responsibility for ethics and regulatory compliance, and control over data and intellectual property subject to applicable agreements. Sponsors should not control interpretation or suppress publication. Positive, negative and inconclusive findings are welcome.

Open science

Encourage preregistration, shared outcome definitions, independent replication, transparent funding disclosures and open-access publication where feasible. Share protocols and aggregate results where permitted; participant data require consent and appropriate governance. Investigators may use Open Science Framework and applicable trial registries. No registry or formal consortium is currently established.

Next steps

Identify qualified investigators, review evidence and feasibility, develop protocols and secure ethics and regulatory approvals before any enrollment.

Research question

Is sustained nightmare relief associated with durable recovery?

Scientific rationale

Patient observations and gaps in existing evidence motivate this hypothesis; efficacy is not established. Review relevant literature.

Proposed methodology

Prospective follow-up with appropriate comparison groups and documentation of concurrent therapy.

Candidate outcomes and limitations

CAPS-5, PCL-5, functioning and persistence of change; confounding and attrition.

Collaboration
